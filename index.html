Who To Upgrade

Only the mask you equip counts, so upgrade the masks you would actually pick for a situation. Each upgrade adds +0.1% to that mask's bonuses and costs 50 coins × its current level. Coins bonuses work everywhere; the other bonuses apply in Arcade only.
